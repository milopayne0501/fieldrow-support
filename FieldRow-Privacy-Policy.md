# FieldRow Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy covers FieldRow version 0.2.0, the Chrome extension provided by the developer identified in its Chrome Web Store listing.

## Information FieldRow handles

FieldRow reads the text you highlight on a webpage only when you click Capture selection. It also handles values you type or paste, column names, unfinished draft values, and records you save. Depending on what you choose, these values may contain names, contact details, or other personal information. Collect only information you are authorized to use, and do not store passwords, credentials, or other sensitive information in FieldRow.

FieldRow temporarily checks the active tab's URL to determine whether it is a supported webpage. It does not record browsing history or automatically attach page URLs to records. URLs you explicitly select, type, or paste are treated like other collection values.

## How information is used and stored

FieldRow uses this information only to let you organize a collection, edit rows and columns, and export saved records as CSV. Draft values and saved records are stored locally in the extension's storage in your Chrome profile. They are not sent to the developer, a server, or third parties. FieldRow does not use Chrome storage sync for this collection.

Text in the selection staging box and unsaved saved-row edits are temporary until assigned or saved. Local storage is not an encrypted vault. Anyone with sufficient access to your device or Chrome profile may be able to access it.

## Sharing, tracking, and limited use

FieldRow has no account system, advertising, analytics, tracking, or background webpage collection. The extension does not transmit your collection externally. Exporting creates a CSV download under your control; any later sharing of that file is your choice.

FieldRow's use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Information is used only for FieldRow's visible collection and CSV features. It is not sold, used for advertising, used to determine creditworthiness or lending eligibility, or made available for the developer or others to read through the extension.

## Retention and control

Saved collection data stays in your Chrome profile until you remove it or uninstall FieldRow. You can edit or delete saved rows, clear the draft, delete columns, or use Start a new collection to remove the collection and return to default columns. Removing a column also removes that column's values. Uninstalling the extension removes its extension storage. Exported CSV files are separate files; deleting extension data does not delete CSV files you downloaded or copies you shared.

## Permissions

- **activeTab:** temporary access to the active webpage after you invoke FieldRow, for manual selection capture and the supported-page check.
- **scripting:** runs a small selection reader on that page when you click Capture selection; no automatic page scanning.
- **storage:** keeps your draft, columns, and records locally between uses.

FieldRow has no persistent website permissions, background service worker, or remote executable code. Form-input, textarea, and editable-field capture is intentionally unsupported.

## Contact and changes

For questions about FieldRow or this policy, use the developer contact details provided on FieldRow's Chrome Web Store listing. Material changes to data handling will be reflected in an updated policy and, where required, communicated before the changed handling begins.
